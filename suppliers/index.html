What “complete” means

A part is complete for listing when it has a name, SKU, category, price and currency, lead time, minimum order quantity, a listing URL, a datasheet URL, at least one spec, an image URL, and a compliance record: an ECCN, an explicit ITAR yes or no, where that classification came from, and a confidence tier of VERIFIED or AUTO_CLASSIFIED. UNKNOWN stays a gap, because it means the classification has not been reviewed.

Subcategory, stock, authorized-distributor status, specs confidence, and how the specs were captured are shown when they are empty. They are optional in the current data model, so they do not block a complete report. Supplier trust is recorded as UNVERIFIED until Xibrary reviews the listing — this form cannot mark your own catalogue verified.

Listing is free while we build. This page is the intake. It does not publish a public parts directory. The catalogue and its gap report stay in this browser (and in a JSON file if you download one) until you clear them.
